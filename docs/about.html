The person behind the pages

I’m Ebin.
Let’s make the digital side feel simpler.

I’m based in Sarnia, Ontario. I help churches, nonprofits, and businesses build a clearer online presence and keep their digital work moving.

I work full-time in project management. That shapes how I approach every website: clear scope, organized execution, and attention to the details that make it useful day to day.

Design matters. So does knowing what happens next, keeping information current, and making the finished website straightforward to maintain.
